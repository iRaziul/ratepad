## What is RatePad?

When traveling abroad, converting price tags should be as fast as pulling out a calculator. Traditional currency apps are often slow, clutter the screen with ads and news, require an active internet connection, or use mid-market rates that ignore your actual exchange fees.

**RatePad** gives you an instant, distraction-free keypad designed for one-handed use in busy markets, grocery stores, and restaurants. Set your bank or exchange booth rate once, and convert prices in real time—even on airplane mode or in remote mountain towns.

## Why Travelers Love RatePad

### ⚡ Lightning-Fast Tactile Keypad
Large, touch-friendly keys engineered for quick price checks while walking or shopping. Supports smooth decimal entry, formatted numeric display, and quick backspace with hold-to-clear.

### 📴 100% Offline Progressive Web App
Install RatePad directly to your home screen on iOS, Android, macOS, or Windows. Everything is cached locally on your device, ensuring full offline capability with zero loading delay.

### 🎯 Your Real Rate, Not Market Fiction
Banks, credit cards, and street exchange booths charge custom rates and fees. RatePad lets you set your exact rate once so every calculation accurately reflects what you actually pay.

### ⇄ Instant Currency Swap
Compare costs in both directions with a single tap. Instantly inverts the exchange rate and swaps source and target currencies.

### 📋 One-Tap Copy
Tap the converted amount to instantly copy it to your clipboard for budget apps, notes, or travel receipts.

### 🌗 Adaptive Light & Dark Mode
Beautiful interface with high-contrast daylight visibility and an OLED-friendly dark mode that respects your system preferences.

### 🛡️ Private & Zero Bloat
- **No ads**
- **No user accounts or logins**
- **No network telemetry or analytics**
- **No subscriptions**
- All custom rates and preferences stay securely in your device's local storage.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

For laptop and desktop power users:

| Key | Action |
|:---|:---|
| **`0` – `9`**, **`.`** | Enter price amount |
| **`Backspace`** | Delete last digit (Hold to clear all) |
| **`C`** / **`Esc`** / **`Delete`** | Clear entered amount |
| **`S`** | Swap source & target currencies |
| **`T`** | Toggle Light / Dark theme |

## Install on Your Device

RatePad runs directly in your browser or installs natively on your home screen:

- **iOS / Safari:** Tap the **Share** button $\rightarrow$ Select **Add to Home Screen** $\rightarrow$ Tap **Add**.
- **Android / Chrome:** Tap the on-screen **Install** prompt banner or open the menu ($\dots$) $\rightarrow$ **Install App**.
- **Desktop (Chrome / Edge / Brave):** Click the **Install** icon in your browser address bar.

---

## Privacy & Security

RatePad never sends your financial calculations or rate preferences across the web. All settings are stored strictly in your browser's private local storage.
